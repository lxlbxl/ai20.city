## 1. Context & thesis

ai20 is an AI implementation + fractional-leadership firm, proven in the EU with a compliance-first wedge (GDPR, EU AI Act). The US is a larger, faster-moving, less regulation-anchored market. **The EU playbook does not port directly** — the compliance wedge that sells in Frankfurt is table-stakes noise in Austin.

**US thesis:** American SMBs and mid-market firms don't buy "AI governance." They buy **more revenue, lower labor cost, and speed** — delivered done-for-you, with regulatory cover only where their industry demands it (healthcare, finance, legal). ai20 wins by being the **outcome-priced, done-for-you AI implementation partner** for regulated and high-labor-cost verticals.

### EU vs US positioning shift

| | EU (`eu.ai20.city`) | US (`us.ai20.city`) |
|---|---|---|
| Lead wedge | Compliance / EU AI Act / GDPR | **ROI, revenue, labor-cost replacement, speed** |
| Hero line | "Silicon Valley velocity with European compliance" | "The AI operating layer for American business. Live in 30 days." |
| Compliance role | Primary selling point | **Risk-remover** per vertical (HIPAA, SOC 2, SEC/FINRA, CCPA, TCPA) — a reason to trust, not the headline |
| Currency | EUR `€` | USD `$` |
| Buyer emotion | "Don't get fined / don't fall behind" | "Grow faster / cut headcount cost / beat competitors" |
| Proof | Governance frameworks | Case-study ROI %, guarantees, speed |

> **Note on "Silicon Valley velocity":** it's a differentiator in Europe; in the US it's non-distinctive (everyone claims it). Replace US messaging with **outcome + done-for-you speed + domestic delivery + industry compliance**.

## 2. Target customer

- **Primary:** US SMB & lower-mid-market, **$1M–$50M revenue**, 10–250 employees, in high-labor-cost or regulated verticals.
- **Economic buyer:** Owner / CEO / COO / practice owner / VP Ops. Not a CIO — these firms have no AI function, which is exactly the gap ai20 fills.
- **Trigger:** drowning in manual work, can't hire fast enough, watching competitors automate, or facing a specific bleed (no-shows, slow lead response, AR aging, support backlog).
- **Why they can't DIY:** no in-house AI talent, no time, fear of getting compliance wrong.

## 4. Business model

Same DIY / DWY / DFY spine as EU, repriced for USD and US willingness-to-pay, but the **go-to-market is a value ladder** designed for land-and-expand and recurring revenue.

### 4.1 Value ladder

| Stage | Offer | Price | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Lead magnet | **AI Readiness Assessment** (existing quiz) | Free | Capture + qualify + score |
| 1. Tripwire | **AI Opportunity Audit** (paid, scoped) | **$1,500–$2,500** | De-risk, diagnose, prescribe. Credited toward build. |
| 2. Core (one-time) | **DFY Implementation** | $5k–$30k+ | Build & deploy the system(s) |
| 3. Continuity (MRR) | **Managed AI retainer** | $500–$5k/mo | Run, optimize, support — the profit engine |
| 4. Ascension | **Fractional CAIO / transformation** | $8k–$20k/mo | Multi-system, strategic, enterprise |

### 4.2 Delivery tiers (per offer)

- **DIY** — setup fee + lower monthly; client operates. Low-touch, self-serve-ish.
- **DWY** — we implement, client co-runs. Mid.
- **DFY** — we build + fully manage. Highest ticket, highest retention. **Push everyone here.**

### 4.3 US-specific pricing principles

- Price in **USD**, premium to EU (US labor being replaced is more expensive → stronger ROI → higher willingness to pay). Rule of thumb: **DFY tiers +20–40% vs EU EUR numerals.**
- **Anchor on replaced cost**, not our cost: a US SDR is $60–80k loaded; a US CSR $45–55k; a medical biller $50k. The offer is "an FTE for a fraction."
- Offer **performance / hybrid pricing** where measurable (US buyers accept it): rev-share or per-booked-meeting on SDR, per-recovered-dollar on AR/collections. Differentiator vs EU.
- **30-Day Implementation Guarantee** on all DFY (we pay your first month if not live) — carried over from EU, strong in US.

### 4.4 Revenue model target

MRR-led. Front-end (audit + build) funds CAC; **managed retainers compound**. North-star: logo → $X one-time build → $Y/mo managed → expansion into 2nd/3rd system → fractional CAIO.

## 8. Compliance guardrails (US)

Not the headline, but must be correct — it's the reason regulated verticals can buy:

- **HIPAA** — BAAs, PHI handling, for healthcare/dental offers.
- **SOC 2** — pursue/represent for finance + enterprise trust.
- **SEC / FINRA** — messaging/record-keeping constraints for financial services offers.
- **TCPA** — consent + opt-out for any SMS/outbound (SDR, receptionist, reminders, RE, home services). Critical — TCPA penalties are per-message.
- **CCPA / CPRA (+ state laws)** — privacy disclosures, data rights, on all lead forms.
- **AI disclosure** — emerging state rules on bot/AI disclosure in customer interactions.
